About

Nice to meet you. I'm Aga.

I'm a certified AuDHD practitioner, systems strategist, and founder of Innovation and Integration. I've been working with neurodivergent professionals, leaders, and organisations since 2010, across Poland, the UK, Brazil, and Singapore. I was late-diagnosed myself, which is why I understand the specific exhaustion of being a high-performing professional who has spent years making themselves fit a world not designed for them.

I work with 2e and neurodivergent professionals, one to one, build teams and support brain-friendly organisational transformation. The work is built around the I&I Framework, which I developed over the past 15 years. It looks at four things: regulation, patterns, meaning, direction.

Alongside coaching, building startups and transforming organisations, I host Brain Friendly Systems, a podcast about how different brains handle work and life.

When not working, I look after my plants, practice yoga, learn about growth from my daughter and try to be present for my family.

I am a coach and educator with years of experience in entrepreneurship education, startup building and innovation. My work is developmental. Where I draw on psychological research, I do so as a practitioner and educator, not a clinician. If you need clinical support, I can help you find it.

Based in Singapore, I speak internationally on neurodivergent leadership and inclusive systems.
